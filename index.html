2. Data we collect ourselves

The App does not require an account and does not transmit your game progress to us. Your save data (progress, upgrades, settings) is stored locally on your device only. If you contact us by email, we process the data contained in your message solely to answer it.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised advertising and any non-essential analytics. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for consent through a Google-certified consent management platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the App's settings.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for in-app purchases and, if you sign in, Play Games features.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalised advertising, security and fraud prevention, and keeping the App stable.

5. Children

AI Tycoon is not directed to children. It is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Retention

Local save data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's own policies. Emails you send us are deleted once your request is resolved, unless retention is legally required.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To exercise your rights, email [SUPPORT-EMAIL].

8. How to delete your data

  • Local progress: delete the App's data in Android settings, or uninstall the App.
  • Advertising: reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device.
  • Play Games data: manage or delete it at myaccount.google.com under Play Games.
  • Or email us and we will help you with deletion.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The current version is always available at this URL, with the date of the last update shown at the top.
